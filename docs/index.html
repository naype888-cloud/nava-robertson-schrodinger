NRS³ — every box has its star

On the box dx × dy × dz each axis carries the pair transport T and position P. At the maximal-tension state Ψ* = ψ*x ⊗ ψ*y ⊗ ψ*z their fluctuations open the angle θNRS(d): zero only with 2 or 3 sites, never zero from 4 on. The quantum lives on boxes with 4 or more sites on every axis; an axis with 2 or 3 sites is marked in red. Change the box and the star deforms; press ▶ and transport moves Ψ through the lattice. The octahedron of the quantum (D25, D37e)

drag to turn · dashed: d → ∞

Each axis with d sites carries the excess over saturation δ(d) = CNava(d) − 1, zero only with 2 or 3 sites (D25). Put a vertex at ±δ on every axis: the octahedron of the box. The regular 4 × 4 × 4 gives the smallest, δ(4) ≈ 0.00848 on every axis; no box reaches the dashed one of d → ∞, δ∞ = C∞ − 1 ≈ 0.13572 on every axis. Its volume is (4/3)·𝒱, with 𝒱 = δ(dx) δ(dy) δ(dz) the volumetric quantum and δ(4)³ ≤ 𝒱 < δ∞³ (D37e).

Speed and the quantum (D44)

|v(t)| against θ(t), one panel per axis

The speed of an axis is v = ((d − 1)/2)·⟨K⟩, K = i[T, P] (D38). Minimum uncertainty (θ = 0) is possible only up to v*(d); on the band Ϙ(d) = (v*(d), 1] every state has a forced defect, and at the cone v = 1 the angle is exactly the quantum θNRS(d). Press Transport: Ψ(t) starts at the quantum and leaves it as it slows down.

Exact in Lean: v*(4) = 3(√5 − 1)/4 (D23f, D23g, D43); the band is empty for 2 or 3 sites and non-empty from 4 on (D44); θ = θNRS(d) at v = 1 (D48). Numerical: v*(d) for d ≥ 5 and the dotted envelope (the smallest angle at each speed). The horizontal scale is stretched above v = 0.9.
