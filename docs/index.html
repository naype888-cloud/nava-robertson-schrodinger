NRS³ on T³ — three closed axes

The three-torus T³ = x × y × z: every axis is closed, three positions with the seam {0, 2}, finite and without boundary. Each axis carries the closed transport T° = (J − I)/2 and the position P₃ = diag(−1, 0, 1). At the maximal current state c every axis resolves 4/5 and keeps 1/5 dark, and Robertson 1934 on the six observables reads det Σ = (5/4)³ |det Ω| = 125/64 |det Ω|. The open cube with three positions keeps nothing dark; closing the axis is what leaves the dark share. Every number below is computed in your browser from the same matrices as in Lean.

Light and dark on every axis

light = |⟨δT°, δP₃⟩|² / (σT² σP²) and dark = 1 − light, for every state (TorusDark.lightShare_add_darkShare). In the band |t| > 1 the dark share is positive for every state (darkShare_pos_of_band); at c it is exactly 1/5 (darkShare_maxState). At tension 1 the axis saturates: dark 0. Transport by T° moves Ψ(t) off c; the orange mark stays at 4/5.

The ladder of Robertson 1934 (Torus.ladder)

At the maximal current state, det Σ / |det Ω|: the open cube saturates (1); each closed axis multiplies by 5/4; T³ gives 125/64, T⁴ gives 625/256, strict from one closed axis on (robertson_det_maxState_strict). For every state with the three axes in the band, entangled or not, the inequality on T³ is strict (TorusStrict.robertson_det_torus_lt). The orange dot is the value at Ψ(t).

Rings of any size

With dx, dy, dz ≥ 3 positions on each closed axis, the transport of the torus is the sum of the three rings. With three positions it is the T° above.

The spectrum is exactly the sums of cosines (TorusSpectrum.eq_lam3_of_eigenvector), so |E| ≤ 3 (TorusSpeed.abs_energy_torus_le_three) and no state becomes orthogonal to itself before π/6 (pi_div_six_le_time_torus); on a single ring the bound is π/2. With even sizes both are attained (TorusSharp.torus_orthogonality_exact, ring_orthogonality_exact). A cut across x crosses a plane and the seam, dy·dz steps each, so S = 2·dy·dz·log 2, an area, independent of the depth (TorusArea.cutEntropy_eq, cutEntropy_eq_of_depth).

Exact in Lean, standard axioms only (repositories nrs3-tritoro on nrs3-hopf):

  • Torus.robertson_det_torus: Robertson 1934 on the torus, every state; Torus.gramDefect_pos: tension above 1 on an axis forces a positive defect, every state.
  • Torus.det_covMatrix_maxState: det Σ = (5/4)^k |det Ω| on T^k; Torus.three_torus: 125/64; Torus.ladder: 1, 5/4, 125/64, 625/256.
  • TorusCommute.torusPairs_comm: different axes commute.
  • TorusStrict.robertson_det_torus_lt: det|NRS³| strict on T³ for every state with the three axes in the band, by the double bracket [[T°, P₃], w₀T° + w₁P₃] ≠ 0.
  • TorusCone.axisVector_mem_futureCone, timelike_of_band: every axis of every state lies in the closed future cone, strictly timelike in the band.
  • TorusDark.lightShare_maxState, darkShare_maxState: 4/5 and 1/5 at c.
  • BoxExcess.variance_TcOp_maxState (9/16), variance_PdOp_maxState (5/6), covariance_maxState (0), ClosedThree.tension_maxState (√6/2), ClosedThree.tension_satState (1).
