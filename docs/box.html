The eight corners: position and the waves of the box

follows the box, Ψ(t) and the turn above · drag any panel to turn all

Everything here is the box above, at the same time: the same sizes, the same Ψ(t), the same turn. The corners {0, d − 1}³ of the open box are not neighbours: there is no seam, each corner has three links, not six. The colour is the staggered sign ε = (−1)x+y+z. On the path it anticommutes with the transport, ε T = −T ε, so (T + mε)² = T² + m², and it pairs the wave k with the wave d + 1 − k, of opposite speed. An open axis has no momentum circle: the waves are the standing waves sin(jkπ/(d+1)), one of each speed, so there are no doublers to count and no Nielsen–Ninomiya sum; the fastest pair, the 8 extreme waves, are the ones ε exchanges. With mass, each wave has the energy ±√(λ² + m²); the closed fibre w (3 positions) added as a fourth axis gives the Kaluza–Klein masses 1, −½, −½ as on the torus. Compare the torus page, where the same 8 corners are one 2 × 2 × 2 cube through the seams.

The octahedron of the quantum (D25, D37e)

drag to turn · dashed: d → ∞

Each axis with d sites carries the excess over saturation δ(d) = CNava(d) − 1, zero only with 2 or 3 sites (D25). Put a vertex at ±δ on every axis: the octahedron of the box. The regular 4 × 4 × 4 gives the smallest, δ(4) ≈ 0.00848 on every axis; no box reaches the dashed one of d → ∞, δ∞ = C∞ − 1 ≈ 0.13572 on every axis. Its volume is (4/3)·𝒱, with 𝒱 = δ(dx) δ(dy) δ(dz) the volumetric quantum and δ(4)³ ≤ 𝒱 < δ∞³ (D37e).

Speed and the quantum (D44)

|v(t)| against θ(t), one panel per axis

The speed of an axis is v = ((d − 1)/2)·⟨K⟩, K = i[T, P] (D38). Minimum uncertainty (θ = 0) is possible only up to v*(d); on the band Ϙ(d) = (v*(d), 1] every state has a forced defect, and at the cone v = 1 the angle is exactly the quantum θNRS(d). Press Transport: Ψ(t) starts at the quantum and leaves it as it slows down.

Exact in Lean: v*(4) = 3(√5 − 1)/4 (D23f, D23g, D43); the band is empty for 2 or 3 sites and non-empty from 4 on (D44); θ = θNRS(d) at v = 1 (D48). Numerical: v*(d) for d ≥ 5 and the dotted envelope (the smallest angle at each speed). The horizontal scale is stretched above v = 0.9.

No windings: the wall does not redden

▶ Bounce or move n

On the open box the light that leaves a galaxy does not come back to it by going around: it reaches the end of the axis and is reflected. There is no link that closes the axis, so no seam, no quantum added per turn and no cover: the images are mirror images inside the box, at distances bounded by d − 1 sites, not new galaxies at d₀ + nL. The spectrum of a state never changes (EventClock.weight_evolve, any state), and with no seam nothing is left dark by a winding: 1 + z = 1 at every bounce. The redshift 1 + z = (5/4)ⁿ of the torus page needs the closed axis; here the same card stays flat, which is the difference of topology.
