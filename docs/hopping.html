RS^N³ on T³ — the hopping, complement of the star

The same torus T³ = dx × dy × dz, read the other way. The star page follows the uncertainty of the pair (T°, P). This page follows the hopping H = −2t T°, the spectral field of that pair. On each closed axis T° has eigenvalues cos(2πm/d), so H has eigenvalues −2t cos(2πm/d), and on the torus E = −2t (cos(2πa/dx) + cos(2πb/dy) + cos(2πc/dz)), with |E| ≤ 6|t|. A mode of H has a sharp energy and a flat position: the dark share on the star page is what that sharpness leaves unresolved. The bar runs from a position eigenstate (s = 0, one site, energy spread over the band) to one hopping mode (s = 1, energy sharp, position spread over the ring). Drag either panel to turn both.

The eight doublers, spectral side: hopping, Dirac and their sum

follows the torus, the mode and Ψ(s) above · drag any panel to turn all

Everything here is the torus above, at the same time: its modes, the occupied mode (○), Ψ(s) (●, at ⟨cos⟩ on each axis, at ⟨cos²⟩ in the squares) and the turn. Every mode (a, b, c) of the torus sits at (cos θa, cos θb, cos θc) in the cube of cosines. The hopping E = −2t Σ cos vanishes on a whole plane, Σ cos = 0. With the Kogut–Susskind signs the three axes anticommute and the energy becomes a length, E = ±√(cos²θa + cos²θb + cos²θc + m²): it vanishes at one point only, the origin, where the eight doublers (±π/2)³ sit. The square root turns the zero plane of the hopping into the zero point of Dirac. Below, their sum: in the squares (cos², cos², cos²) the Dirac energy squared is the sum of the three axes, D² = Tx² + Ty² + Tz² + m². The fibre of 3 positions gives the mass ½.

Hopping: E = −2t Σ cos, zero on a plane

Dirac: E = ±√(Σ cos² + m²), zero at one point

Sum: the squares, E² − m² = cos² + cos² + cos²

Lean (nrs3-tritoro): TorusDirac3.dirac3_sq (Dm² = Tx² + Ty² + Tz² + m² on T³, even sizes), the anticommutations Tx_Ay, Tx_Az, Ay_Az, eps_Tx, eps_Ay, eps_Az, and dirac3_wave (the eight waves (±i)x(±i)y(±i)z are zero modes of D₀, and the mass sends each to the opposite corner). The spectrum of each ring is the Lean TorusSpectrum.eq_cos_of_eigenvector. The fibre and its mass are numerical.

The hopping band of every ring

Each ring of d positions has hopping eigenvalues −2t cos(2πm/d), m = 0, …, d − 1. They fill [−2|t|, 2|t|] and touch both ends: m = 0 gives −2t, and for even d the alternating mode gives +2t. ○ the occupied mode · ● ⟨H⟩ of Ψ(s), with the spread σH. At s = 0 the state is one site and ⟨H⟩ = 0 with the spread open. At s = 1 the dot sits on the mode and the spread closes. Press Tour to stay on one torus, Hop to pause.

The octahedron of the band

drag to turn · dashed: the band edge

Put a vertex at ±1 on every axis: that octahedron is the whole cosine band, |λ| ≤ 1 on each ring, the same bound for every size (TorusSpeed.abs_energy_le_one). The inner octahedron has vertices at the occupied |cos(2πm/d)|. On the canonical 3 × 3 × 3 the modes are 1 and −1/2, so a single axis occupies either the outer vertex or 1/2. The star page draws δ, the unresolved share. This octahedron draws the resolved spectrum.

Energy spread against position spread

σH against σP, one panel per axis

The complement of the tension plot. As s runs from the site to the mode, σH falls and σP rises. At s = 1 the mode is an eigenvector of H, so σH = 0 and the position fills the ring. At s = 0 the site is an eigenvector of P, so σP = 0 and the energy fills the neighbours. The product stays positive in between: concentrating one observable opens the other.

Exact in Lean for the closed transport, standard axioms only (nrs3-tritoro): the spectrum of T° on a ring is {cos(2πm/d)} (TorusSpectrum.eq_cos_of_eigenvector, ring_mulVec_wave), and on T³ it is cos(2πa/dx) + cos(2πb/dy) + cos(2πc/dz) (TorusSpectrum.eq_lam3_of_eigenvector, T3c_mulVec_wave3), with |E| ≤ 1 on a ring and |E| ≤ 3 on the torus (TorusSpeed.abs_energy_le_one, abs_energy_torus_le_three). The hopping on this page is the same matrix scaled, H = −2t T°, so its eigenvalues are −2t times those cosines and |E| ≤ 6|t|. The numbers are computed in the browser from those cosines.
