RS^N³ on the open box — the hopping, complement of the star

The same open box dx × dy × dz, read the other way. Each axis is a chain of d positions joined by d − 1 links, the transport Td against the position Pd; there is no seam and no link between the two ends. The star page follows the uncertainty of the pair (T°, P). This page follows the hopping H = −2t T°, the spectral field of that pair. On each open axis T° has eigenvalues cos(πk/(d+1)), k = 1, …, d (standing waves sin(jkπ/(d+1))), so H has eigenvalues −2t cos(πk/(d+1)), and on the box E = −2t (cos(πa/(dx+1)) + cos(πb/(dy+1)) + cos(πc/(dz+1))), with |E| < 6|t|, never reached. A standing wave of H has a sharp energy and a spread position. The bar runs from a position eigenstate at the end of the chain (s = 0, one site, energy spread over the band) to one standing wave (s = 1, energy sharp, position spread over the axis). Drag either panel to turn both.

The hopping band of every axis

Each open axis of d positions has hopping eigenvalues −2t cos(πk/(d+1)), k = 1, …, d. They are symmetric about 0 and stay strictly inside (−2|t|, 2|t|): the edge 2|t| is the limit of the infinite chain, never reached by a finite one. ○ the occupied mode · ● ⟨H⟩ of Ψ(s), with the spread σH. At s = 0 the state is one end site and the spread is open. At s = 1 the dot sits on the mode and the spread closes. Press Tour to stay on one box, Hop to pause.

Put a vertex at ±1 on every axis: that octahedron is the whole cosine band, |λ| < 1 on each open axis, the same bound for every size. The inner octahedron has vertices at the occupied |cos(πk/(d+1))|. On the canonical 3 × 3 × 3 the modes are ±1/√2 and 0, so a single axis occupies either 1/√2 or the centre. The star page draws δ, the unresolved share. This octahedron draws the resolved spectrum.

The complement of the tension plot. As s runs from the end site to the standing wave, σH falls and σP rises. At s = 1 the mode is an eigenvector of H, so σH = 0 and the position fills the axis. At s = 0 the site is an eigenvector of P, so σP = 0 and the energy fills the neighbours. The product stays positive in between: concentrating one observable opens the other.
